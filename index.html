Requirements

The closed beta gave us signal. These insights shape the launch approach.

The HR Leader is the audience.
The PI Champion is the activator and the carrier. Managers receive her coaching — they're not the primary audience of this package.
Partners need to look like experts.
They can't be expected to be on-the-fly prompt writers. Partners need to feel confident bringing the Assistant into a client engagement.
Activation is a blank-page problem.
Users don't know where to start — which is why live consultative calls have been driving closed beta activation. The package solves that at scale.
